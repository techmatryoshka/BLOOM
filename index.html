About Bloom

Bloom is free and always will be.
No ads, no paywalls.
Enjoy your growth, carefree.

Bloom was designed for neurodivergent minds by a neurodivergent mind — because you need a little gentleness and grace to get going.

Bloom is not here to turn you into a productive machine. She asks you to tend to one thing, like a gardener taking care of one flower at a time. She doesn't use lists or overwhelming timers that fill your screen — she encourages your growth with care.

XP & Growth System

Every session you complete earns you XP. Watch yourself grow from a Seedling all the way to Fairy Queen — because every task you finish, no matter how small, is worth celebrating.

Daily Bloom Log

A gentle daily record of everything you tended to, with space to write your own thoughts. Your data stays on your device — private, always.

Lo-fi Focus Radio

Ambient music streams softly in the background while you work, because the right sounds can make even the hardest tasks feel softer.

Meet the Designer

Amina Radončić

Neuroadaptive System Engineer

Hi! I'm Amina — a neuroadaptive system engineer who designs whimsical systems for neurodivergent minds. I hold an MSc in Bioengineering and am currently a PhD student in Electrical Engineering and IT.

As a person with AuDHD who constantly struggled with focus and time blindness, I decided to create Bloom for minds alike — hoping that it would inspire you to fulfill the tasks that feel impossible to start.

"One flower at a time."
