About Bloom

Bloom is free and always will be.
No ads, no paywalls.
Enjoy your growth, carefree.

Bloom was designed for neurodivergent minds by a neurodivergent mind — because you need a little gentleness and grace to get going. She is not here to turn you into a productive machine. She asks you to tend to one thing, like a gardener taking care of one flower at a time.

XP & Growth System

Every focus session and journal entry earns you XP. Grow from Seedling to Fairy Queen across 7 levels — each unlocking new affirmations.

Daily Bloom Log & Journal

A gentle daily record of everything you tended to, plus a personal journal that resets every 24 hours. Writing earns you XP too.

Rest Mode

Rest is not giving up — it is how you bloom. Timed rest sessions earn you XP and count toward your streak. You deserve to rest.

Nature Soundscapes

Rain, forest, birds, stream, wind, fireplace — six ambient soundscapes generated in real time to help with sensory regulation and focus.

Week in Bloom

A weekly view of your focus time, streak, XP, ladybug bloom badges, and journal highlights. See your patterns and celebrate your growth.

Lo-fi Focus Radio

Four music styles including Drone Zone, Groove Salad, and a Chillhop lo-fi link. Draggable player goes wherever you like.

Privacy First

Everything stays on your device. No accounts, no tracking, no servers. Export your data anytime to back it up — it belongs to you.

Seasonal Themes

Bloom changes with the seasons — cherry blossoms in spring, daisies in summer, maple leaves in autumn, snowflakes in winter.

Meet the Designer

Amina Radončić

Neuroadaptive System Engineer

Hi! I'm Amina — a neuroadaptive system engineer who designs whimsical systems for neurodivergent minds. I hold an MSc in Bioengineering and am currently a PhD student in Electrical Engineering and IT.

As a person with AuDHD who constantly struggled with focus and time blindness, I decided to create Bloom for minds alike — hoping that it would inspire you to fulfill the tasks that feel impossible to start.

"One flower at a time."
